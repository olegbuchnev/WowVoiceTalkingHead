Russian audio for Forever quests, bundled with WowVoice TalkingHead.

Source: CatQuest and CatQuest_Voices 0.2.0, by Cathey (daniilcathey).
Author: https://t.me/catheyco
Imported from the locally supplied pack; the OGG recordings are unmodified.
All quests present in WowVoice's Classic pack are excluded, across all sections.
Gender-specific recordings are selected for the player's character.
Some Forever turn-ins use temporary translations from English, as noted in
the source README; their wording may differ from the Russian game client.

The source CatQuest README describes the pack as a noncommercial fan project,
generated with F5-TTS (Russian fine-tuning, CC BY-NC) using NPC voice references
from the game, and attributes the original texts and voices to Blizzard.
