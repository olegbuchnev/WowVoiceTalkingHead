talking-heads.png contains Blizzard Entertainment's World of Warcraft UI artwork.
Source: https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-textures/blob/live/QUESTFRAME/TalkingHeads.PNG
The same artwork is used by src/Media/TalkingHeads.tga in the addon.
The artwork belongs to Blizzard Entertainment; it is not original TalkingHead Ru artwork.

welcome-adventurers.png is unmodified Blizzard Entertainment character artwork
from the official World of Warcraft Recruit A Friend page:
https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/game/recruit-a-friend
Original asset (transparent PNG):
https://blz-contentstack-images.akamaized.net/v3/assets/blt3452e3b114fab0cd/bltdc2f76ae58a5627e/5db72f7213b457491b4be503/1or3_card_MultipleFriends_desktop.png
The artwork belongs to Blizzard Entertainment.

portrait-placeholder.png is Blizzard's INV_Misc_Note_01 icon, also used by
src/Portrait.lua while the game portrait loads (64x64 pixels).
Source: https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-textures/blob/live/ICONS/INV_Misc_Note_01.PNG

addon-icon.png is copied from docs/images/addon-icon.png in this repository:
the TalkingHead Ru addon portrait, containing Blizzard's dwarf character artwork.

addon-icon-cutout.png is a transparent derivative of addon-icon.png, edited with
imagegen to remove the background and outer frame for the site header and favicon.

NPC models/textures are sourced from wow.zamimg.com/modelviewer/classicplus/;
ZamModelViewer is sourced from its live branch. The server caches these assets
and the viewer stylesheet outside the application image and Git repository.
Viewer notices: https://wow.zamimg.com/modelviewer/live/viewer/viewer.min.js.LICENSE.txt

fonts/frizqt___cyr.ttf and fonts/morpheus_cyr.ttf were extracted from the
locally installed World of Warcraft Forever client 1.60.1.70338 (ruRU),
using CASCConsole v1.0.240. FileDataIDs: 615971 and 615976 respectively.
These are original game font assets for this local prototype, not original
TalkingHead Ru fonts. All font rights remain with their respective owners.
