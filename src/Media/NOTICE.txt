TalkingHeads.tga contains Blizzard Entertainment's World of Warcraft UI artwork.
Source: https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-textures/blob/live/QUESTFRAME/TalkingHeads.PNG
Converted from PNG to uncompressed 32-bit TGA without changing the pixels.
The artwork belongs to Blizzard Entertainment; it is not original WowVoice TalkingHead artwork.

QueueClose.png is an unchanged copy of EllesmereUI/media/icons/eui-close.png.
Source: the locally installed EllesmereUI addon. Used for quest queue removal controls.
This asset is not original WowVoice TalkingHead artwork.

Play.tga and PlaySelected.tga are original geometric circular playback buttons
generated by tools/build-play-icon.js. Both are 128x128 grayscale icons with
supersampled edges, a beveled rim, and transparent surroundings; the selected
variant uses a bright filled disc. No artwork from other addons was copied.

QueueNext.tga and QueueCheck.tga are original geometric queue control icons
generated by tools/build-queue-icons.js. Both are 32x32 white icons with
supersampled transparent edges, tinted to match the control text in game.

VoiceChat-Active.tga combines Blizzard's standard voice chat speaker and waves.
Sources:
https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-textures/blob/live/COMMON/VOICECHAT-SPEAKER.PNG
https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-textures/blob/live/COMMON/VOICECHAT-ON.PNG
Both 32x32 images are composited at their original coordinates, speaker first,
then waves using source-over alpha blending, without drawing new artwork.
The visible artwork occupies x=0..21, y=3..19. A centered 22x22 crop at
(0, 0.5) is uniformly resized to 32x32 using high-quality bicubic interpolation
and half-pixel sampling. This removes excess transparent padding, fills the
icon width and centers the speaker without changing its proportions.
Saved as uncompressed 32-bit BGRA TGA with a top-left origin and 8-bit alpha.
The addon references this shared texture in TalkingHeadRu/Media.
The artwork belongs to Blizzard Entertainment; it is not original WowVoice TalkingHead artwork.

VoteAdventurers.tga contains the unchanged Recruit A Friend artwork used by
web/voice-vote/public/welcome-adventurers.png. tools/convert-vote-art.ps1 packages
the original 346x290 pixels in a transparent 512x512 uncompressed BGRA TGA.
Source: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/game/recruit-a-friend
Original asset:
https://blz-contentstack-images.akamaized.net/v3/assets/blt3452e3b114fab0cd/bltdc2f76ae58a5627e/5db72f7213b457491b4be503/1or3_card_MultipleFriends_desktop.png
The artwork belongs to Blizzard Entertainment.

Fonts/frizqt___cyr.ttf is the same original Blizzard Russian Friz font as on the

615971). It is used by the independent voting invitation on all client locales.
